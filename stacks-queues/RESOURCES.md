# Stacks, Queues, Deques, and Priority Queues Resources

## Knowledge

- Local file: `~/school/c949/C949_Data_Structures_and_Algorithms_1_Study_Guide.md` (outside this repo)
  The practice exam content. Ground truth for what C949 asks. Use for: scoping every lesson to exam question types.
- [algs4: Section 1.3, Bags, Queues, and Stacks (Sedgewick & Wayne, Princeton)](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/13stacks/)
  The canonical university treatment: the stack's push/pop/top contract, the queue's enqueue/dequeue/front contract, and what each principle (LIFO, FIFO) means. Use for: the lesson 1 definitions.
- [algs4: Section 2.4, Priority Queues (Sedgewick & Wayne, Princeton)](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/24pq/)
  The canonical treatment of priority queues: removing the highest-priority item and the operations that support it. Use for: the lesson 2 priority queue definitions.
- Book: _Introduction to Algorithms_ (CLRS) - Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein
  Chapter 10 covers stacks, queues, and deques; Chapter 6 covers priority queues and heaps. Use for: settling any dispute about definitions.
- [Wikipedia: Double-ended queue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-ended_queue)
  The deque concept: insertion and removal at both ends. Use for: the lesson 2 deque definition.
