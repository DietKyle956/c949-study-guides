How the review works

Test 1 diagnoses. Work it once, all forty questions, then use every miss to reopen that topic’s lessons - the miss names the topic you need, and the lesson names the rule that answers it.

Test 2 proves transfer. Take it in a later session with the same emphasis and entirely different questions; the score should hold. When it does, you are recognizing rules rather than memorizing examples.

Space it out. Redo test 1 after two days and test 2 the morning before the exam. If a test starts to feel familiar, ask your agent for fresh variants.
