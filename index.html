How the site works

  • One path. The nineteen lessons form a single path, starting with Big O and ending with Huffman coding. Each lesson ends with previous and next buttons that name the next lesson, and the last lesson hands off to practice test 1.
  • Drill as you read. Every lesson ends in a quiz with instant feedback - answer, see why, move on.
  • Carry the cards. Each topic has a one-page cheat card for the rules and definitions. Keep them open while drilling until recognition is automatic.
  • Finish with the tests. Two 40-question interleaved practice tests mix every topic with no labels, exam-weighted. Test 1 diagnoses; test 2 proves the rules transferred.
