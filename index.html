Big O for Exams

A short course on stating the time and space complexity of code - loops, recursion, and the traps exam writers love. Each lesson teaches one idea, shows worked examples, then drills it with a quiz.

Lessons

LessonWhat you learnStatus
1 - The Loop Rules Multiply, add, halve: a three-rule procedure that solves any loop-based complexity question. Ready
2 - Recursion Count the levels, count the work per level, handle branching calls. Ready
3 - Space complexity Auxiliary space: growing containers, the recursion stack, and the traps. Ready
4 - Recurrence relations Count the levels for any shrink: T(N) = N + T(N - 8) has about N / 8 levels. Ready
5 - Mixed exam review Exam-style questions mixing every lesson, plus the leftover tricky patterns. Ready
